Overview

Autonomous Product Operations Agent is an AI-powered SaaS platform designed to automate Agile product workflows, sprint planning, Jira coordination, stakeholder reporting, and operational analytics using LLMs, RAG pipelines, and workflow automation systems.

The platform helps product managers, engineering teams, and startups streamline operational processes and improve product delivery efficiency.
